# Code of Conduct

Kothalipi brings together people from many countries, languages and backgrounds.
We want everyone to feel welcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and non-native English speakers.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Respect every language and dialect. No mocking of accents or spelling.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks
- Sharing anyone's voice recordings or personal data without consent
- Spam, trolling, or sustained disruption

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers (see the organization profile for contact).
Maintainers may remove content, warn, or ban contributors who break these rules.

This Code of Conduct is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
